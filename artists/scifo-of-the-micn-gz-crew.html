Scifo of the Micn Gz Crew

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Scifo, born Andil Houlder, is a Toronto rapper best known as one half of the Canadian hip-hop duo Mic'N Gz Crew. The group emerged from Toronto's early-1990s rap scene and released All Chix Lov Da Mix through the Canadian label Def Beat Records. Northern Dial carries Scifo through his later guest appearance on 2 Versatile's 'Guiding Light.'
