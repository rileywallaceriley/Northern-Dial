dj charlie b

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Ontario-born Ajay 'Charlie B' Saxena is a Canadian DJ, producer and music executive known for championing Toronto and Canadian hip-hop. A JUNO-nominated artist and longtime collaborator with artists including Pressa and Preme, he has also served as Director of A&R at Warner Music Canada and built a reputation as 'Mr. Canada.'
