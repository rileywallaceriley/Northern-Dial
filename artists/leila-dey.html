leila dey

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Jamaican-born, Toronto-raised Canadian R&B singer-songwriter Leila Dey blends soulful vocals with hip-hop, reggae and jazz influences. A longtime fixture in Toronto’s R&B scene, she has released projects including DETOUR, opened for artists such as Jessie Reyez and built a catalogue rooted in vulnerable, cinematic songwriting.
