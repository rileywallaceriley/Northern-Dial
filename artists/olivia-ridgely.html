olivia ridgely

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Olivia Ridgely is a Montreal-based Canadian indie-pop singer and songwriter whose catalogue includes releases such as Busy Body, Flowers for a Friend, Blu/Lucky, Hot Cakes and twenty18. Her work also extends into collaborations across Canadian pop, folk and electronic music, including songs with Theo Day and Montreal artists. Northern Dial carries Olivia Ridgely as part of its contemporary Canadian indie-pop catalogue.
