la dynastie des morniers

Montréal, Quebec, Canada

La Dynastie des Morniers is a Montréal hip-hop collective closely connected to Muzion and the city's Saint-Michel rap community. Emerging from the same mid-1990s network that produced Wolfpack, Akadémia and Muzion, the collective included Montréal rappers such as Le Voyou and appeared on Muzion's 2002 album J'Rêvolutionne.
