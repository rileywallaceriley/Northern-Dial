pluto

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Pluto was a Vancouver alternative-rock band formed in 1993 by Ian Jones, Rolf Hetherington, John Ounpuu and Justin Leigh. After early independent and Mint Records releases, the group signed with Virgin Records and earned a 1997 Juno nomination for Best New Group. Paste, from the band's 1996 self-titled album, is the Pluto recording in the Northern Dial library.
