lil darklord

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

LIL Darklord is an alias of Toronto producer and artist Jahmar Carter, also known as Daxz. Carter emerged from Toronto's hip-hop production scene and has credits connected to Drake, KILLY, The Weeknd and other major artists. Slump City, his 2018 collaboration with WondaGurl from the Yesterday project, is the LIL Darklord credit in the Northern Dial library.
