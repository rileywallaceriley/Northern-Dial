ro dolla

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Ro Dolla is a Toronto artist, producer and creative known for his work with The Circle and the R&B and hip-hop group Big Black Lincoln. A longtime figure in Toronto music, he has collaborated with artists including Saukrates and Choclair while extending his creative work beyond recording. Northern Dial carries Ro Dolla as part of its Toronto hip-hop and R&B history catalogue.
