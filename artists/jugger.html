jugger

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

JUGGER is a Toronto hip-hop artist whose dark, high-energy rap catalogue spans solo work and a long-running creative partnership with Toronto producer WondaGurl. Their 2025 project BABA YAGA 2 includes several of JUGGER's six Northern Dial library tracks, alongside singles such as DIPSET and HELTER SKELTER.
