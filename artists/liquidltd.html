liquidltd

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

LiquidLtd is a Montreal-linked music project associated with vocalist and founding member Stephen 'Liquid' Hawley. Hawley is based in Montreal, Quebec, and the project maintains an independent catalogue spanning releases and online performances. Northern Dial carries LiquidLtd as part of its Canadian independent music catalogue.
