Culture

Canada

Culture is a Canadian-Bahamian rapper and reggae artist best known for appearing on Karl Wolf's hit cover of Africa. His work sits at the intersection of Canadian hip-hop, reggae and pop, with Africa reaching the upper tier of the Canadian Hot 100 and bringing his voice to a national audience. Northern Dial carries Culture as part of its Canadian hip-hop and reggae catalogue.
