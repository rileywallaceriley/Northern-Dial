cyrus robertson-orkish

Ottawa, Ontario, Canada

Cape Breton-born musician, songwriter and producer Cyrus Robertson-Orkish has built a versatile Canadian career spanning alternative rock, progressive music, jazz fusion and experimental pop. After establishing himself in Halifax with Dali Van Gogh and solo work as Cyrus R.O., he later became active in Ottawa through projects including Finely Tuned Elephant, GirlBoyThing and Victor Complex.
