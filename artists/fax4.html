fax4

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Fax4 is a Halifax hip-hop crew made up of Spesh K, Quake Matthews, Boy-Ill and Markit. Active in Nova Scotia's mid-2000s rap scene, the group released the 2006 album Inklined and became part of the broader East Coast Canadian underground alongside artists including Classified and Mic Boyd.
