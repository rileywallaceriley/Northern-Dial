Punchinrd

Brampton, Ontario, Canada

Punchinrd is a Canadian hip-hop artist associated with Brampton-rooted Capital Vision and a network of emerging Peel Region rappers. His independent catalogue includes 'Believe In Me,' 'I Am Punchin,' 'Married to the Game' and 'Expensive Dinner,' while Capital Vision has also promoted his releases directly. Northern Dial carries Punchinrd as part of its contemporary Brampton-area rap catalogue.
