chase the bear

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Vancouver six-piece Chase the Bear is a Canadian rock band known for high-energy, hook-heavy songs and a hard-driving live show. Signed to Rock Is Dead Records/604 Records, the group released its debut album Honey in 2023 and followed it with new singles and the Demons EP.
