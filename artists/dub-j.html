dub j

Kitchener-Waterloo, Ontario, Canada

Dub J is a Canadian producer and songwriter from Kitchener-Waterloo whose career spans more than two decades. He has worked with Canadian artists including Classified, JD Era, Maestro Fresh Wes, Jully Black, Choclair, Saukrates and Paris Richards, and became known for large-scale collaboration projects such as Love 2 Hate. Northern Dial carries Dub J as part of Canada's hip-hop production history.
