lincoln

Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

Lincoln Blaché is a Juno-nominated Canadian recording artist, songwriter and producer from Mississauga. He first gained wider recognition through his featured vocal on Doman & Gooding's dance single Runnin', then continued into independent music and artist development as founder of MADE IN SAUGA. Runnin' is the Lincoln credit in the Northern Dial library.
