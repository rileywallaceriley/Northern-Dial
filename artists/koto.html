koto?

London, Ontario, Canada

KOTO? is a London, Ontario-rooted rapper and producer active in Toronto and a member of the 4DaTribe collective. His music draws heavily on boom-bap and lo-fi hip-hop, pairing compact, sample-driven production with dense lyricism across projects including Mornington Me and 4DaTribe Disc Two. DerekFisher, with Soft Eyez and Chai Sully, is his Northern Dial library credit.
