private name private number

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Private Name Private Number, also known as PNPN, is a Toronto hip-hop artist, songwriter, producer and independent label owner whose music blends rap, R&B and Caribbean influence. MusicOntario has presented him as a Canadian artist and HipHopCanada has documented his Toronto-rooted catalogue through releases including Terminal 3 and collaborations with Rochester and Choclair. Seven Private Name Private Number tracks appear in the Northern Dial library.
