gray rowan

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Gray Rowan is a Toronto producer and musician active across the city's R&B, electronic and independent music scenes. Rowan has co-produced work for artists including Maddee and contributed to recordings with Matthew Progress, Dan Only, Kingo Halla and Daniela Andrade, while also working from the NDG 208 studio in Toronto's east end. Northern Dial carries Gray Rowan as part of its Canadian producer and collaborator catalogue.
