Jake Soberlak

Jake Soberlak is a young blues guitarist, singer and harmonica player from Kamloops, British Columbia. His debut album, I'm In Trouble, arrived September 11, 2026 after an early run of live shows and the single “You Gotta Leave.”

The record was made live-off-the-floor with a full band at Afterlife Studios in Vancouver, giving Soberlak’s traditional blues foundation a direct, unpolished feel.
