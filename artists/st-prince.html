st-prince

Québec City, Quebec, Canada

Québec City rapper St-Prince, born Brandon Truong, is part of the new generation of francophone urban artists blending melodic trap, contemporary R&B and pop. After emerging with the Sosluv collective, he moved into solo work, signed with Disques 7ième Ciel and released the 2026 EP rien de saint, alongside collaborations with Koriass, FouKi, High Klassified and others.
