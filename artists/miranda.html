miranda

Ontario, Canada

Miranda is a Canadian R&B vocalist associated with producer Dub J's Q It Up Records network and frequently credited as part of Mystic & Miranda. Her work appears within a Canadian hip-hop and R&B catalogue that connected artists across Kitchener-Waterloo and Toronto. Northern Dial carries Miranda as part of that early-2000s Canadian scene.
