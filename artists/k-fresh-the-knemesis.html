k fresh the knemesis

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto rapper, songwriter and producer K-Fresh, later known as Knemesis, has worked across music and visual production in the city’s hip-hop scene. His early credits include the posse cut Get It Right from JB’s My Environment before he continued releasing solo material and directing music videos under the Knemesis name.
