k.c. thomas

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

K.C. Thomas is a Toronto hip-hop artist connected to the Circle collective and Choclair's early independent catalogue. K.C. Thomas appears alongside Afrolistic on What It Takes (Ill B. Mix), a key recording from Toronto's 1990s rap scene. Northern Dial carries K.C. Thomas as part of that early Canadian hip-hop lineage.
