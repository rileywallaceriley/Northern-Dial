monark

Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada

Mister Monark is a Fredericton, New Brunswick hip-hop artist associated with Atlantic Canada's long-running underground rap scene. His work includes collaborations with artists such as Phakt and Sean One, and he has appeared on projects and compilations documenting Canadian rap from Fredericton and Saint John. Northern Dial carries Monark as part of its East Coast Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
