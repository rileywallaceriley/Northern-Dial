lan'do

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Lan'do is a Canadian-born rapper and songwriter who was raised in Jamaica before returning to a Toronto-rooted music community. His genre-blurring solo work draws on hip-hop, dancehall and international influences, and he has also collaborated with Toronto collective No Tourists. His appearance on X10Land's 2023 single Walking With Wolves with Junia-T, Private Name Private Number, Victory and Patrik Kabongo is his Northern Dial library credit.
