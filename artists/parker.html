parker

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

Scarborough MC Parker is a member of Monolith, the Canadian hip-hop collective formed in Toronto in the mid-1990s. He appears with the crew on recordings including Wio-K’s Ten and Grimace Love’s Perception-era material, part of the interconnected catalogue that made Monolith a fixture of Toronto underground rap.
