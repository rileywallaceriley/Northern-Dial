Sugar Prince

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Sugar Prince is a Canadian old-school hip-hop artist associated with Vancouver's early-2000s rap scene. He appears with Jah-Fus on the Rascalz track 'Clash (We Don't Play)' from the Vancouver group's 2002 album 'Reloaded' and is catalogued among Canadian old-school hip-hop artists. Northern Dial carries Sugar Prince as part of its classic Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
