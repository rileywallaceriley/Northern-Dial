GlomanOnThaBeat

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

GlomanOnThaBeat is a Toronto producer and recording artist whose independent catalogue spans hip-hop and beat-driven releases. He has collaborated with Canadian artists including Vancouver's Illyminiachi and has released projects such as Today and How 2. Northern Dial carries GlomanOnThaBeat as part of its Toronto hip-hop and producer catalogue.
