freakey

Montréal, Quebec, Canada

FREAKEY! is a Montréal producer and artist from the Saint-Léonard and Repentigny areas whose futuristic hip-hop production has reached artists in Canada, the United States and Europe. Raised in a Haitian family in Montréal, he has produced for artists including Don Toliver, Hamza, KILLY and Lost while developing his own solo catalogue. His 2020 collaboration EUPHORIA with Toronto producer WondaGurl is his Northern Dial library credit.
