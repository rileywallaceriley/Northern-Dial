Toronto, Ontario · Pop-Punk / Alt-Pop

LØLØ

LØLØ is a Toronto singer-songwriter whose music sits between pop-punk, alternative pop and diaristic guitar music. She built her audience through sharply written breakup songs and a self-aware visual style before expanding into larger tours and label releases.

Her 2026 run includes new songs such as “007,” “the punisher” and “pretty little wreck,” plus a headline North American tour that returns to Toronto at The Opera House on September 17.
