ANJXLXE

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

ANJXLXE (Anne-Julie) is a Montreal-based singer-songwriter whose music bridges contemporary R&B, soul and pop in French and English. Her debut album ANJXLXE arrived in 2025, earning an ADISQ nomination for R&B/Soul Album of the Year, while singles including Old School Love and Destin helped establish her within Quebec's modern R&B scene. Northern Dial carries ANJXLXE as part of its contemporary Montreal R&B catalogue.
