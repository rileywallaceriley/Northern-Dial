robb klarke

Milton, Ontario, Canada

Robb Klarke is a Milton, Ontario-based multidisciplinary artist and musician connected to Canadian hip-hop. His work includes visual and animation projects for Toronto rap artists as well as a featured performance with Saukrates and Dankery Harv on My Luv from Saukrates' Amani deluxe EP. Northern Dial carries Robb Klarke as part of its Greater Toronto Area hip-hop and multidisciplinary arts catalogue.
