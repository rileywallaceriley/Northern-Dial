646yf4t

Oshawa, Ontario, Canada

646yf4t, pronounced Babyfat, is Oshawa, Ontario singer, songwriter and producer Zachary Simmonds. The younger brother of Daniel Caesar, he moved from production into his own genre-blurring blend of alternative R&B, punk and soul with the 2025 project Growing Pains before appearing on Caesar's Son of Spergy.
