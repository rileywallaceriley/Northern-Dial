ayoo bigz

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

AYOO BIGZ is a Toronto underground rapper whose career stretches from early mixtapes made on a karaoke machine to a return to recording after a long hiatus. Rooted in East Toronto hip-hop, his hard-edged catalogue includes collaborations with Canadian artists such as Merkules, Sayzee, Daniel Son and Tona as well as international underground rap veterans.
