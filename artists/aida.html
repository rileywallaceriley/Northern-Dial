aida

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Aïda is a Montreal-based Canadian singer-songwriter whose music blends pop, R&B and multicultural influences with a bilingual Frenglish identity. Rooted in Montreal's diverse music scene and African heritage, her work moves across languages and styles while centring a contemporary Quebec perspective. Northern Dial carries Aïda as part of Montreal's independent vocal scene.
