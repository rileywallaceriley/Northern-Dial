vince maria

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Vince 'Vinny Mac' Maria is a Canadian producer, songwriter and musician whose career developed through Toronto's dance and remix scene. A five-time Juno nominee, he has produced and remixed work for Canadian and international artists and is a member of the Canadian dance project Hellbent. The Ryder Remix of Hellbent's Fast Car is his Northern Dial library credit.
