xentury

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Xentury is a Toronto hip-hop and R&B artist whose independent catalogue connects her with the city's rap and soul community. She has recorded across the Greater Toronto Area with artists including Dan-e-o, Michie Mee and DJ Mercilless while developing her own releases and XI Entertainment. Her appearance with Masta Ace on Dan-e-o's 2024 single Cleanse My Soul is her Northern Dial library credit.
