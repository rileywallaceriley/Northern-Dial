Northern Dial Artist Profile

heybombay

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

HeyBombay, also known as Bombay, is a Qatar-born music producer residing in Toronto, Canada. Working across hip-hop, R&B, neo-soul and beat music, he became a key collaborator of Toronto artist Ice Tha One, producing her MONSTAR material and later sharing artist billing on the deluxe edition. Their Dance Like Me collaboration with Truss and Junia-T is HeyBombay's Northern Dial library credit.
