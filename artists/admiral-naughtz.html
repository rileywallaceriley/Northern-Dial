admiral naughtz

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Admiral Naughtz is a Toronto underground hip-hop MC associated with the city's hardcore and boom-bap scene and the Hella Treez circle. Active on Toronto bills for years, he has collaborated with D.O.V and DJ Chino420; his feature on D.O.V's Back From The Dead is the Northern Dial library credit, while U.G.M.L. extends that affiliation into Hella Treez's current catalogue.
