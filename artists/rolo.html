Rolo

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Rolo is a Toronto underground hip-hop artist represented on the 1998 local compilation Invading the Mega City. The release credits Rolo with King David on 'Warriors Come Thru,' while Northern Dial also carries Rolo's 'In T.O.' The compilation documents Toronto-based independent rap artists from the era.
