naïma frank

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Montreal singer-songwriter Naïma Frank is a rising voice in Quebec's francophone R&B scene, blending alternative R&B, pop, electro, hip-hop and jazz influences with writing shaped by her Haitian heritage. Her debut album 18 ans et + arrived in 2026 after the acclaimed EP Petite fille devient grande.
