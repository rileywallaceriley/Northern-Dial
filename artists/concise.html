concise

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Vancouver MC and producer Concise the Black Knight is a longtime figure in the city's hip-hop community. Born in North Africa and raised in Vancouver after immigrating to Canada, he came up alongside Checkmate, appeared in the Northern Touch era and built a solo catalogue rooted in independent West Coast rap.
