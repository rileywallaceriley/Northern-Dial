kxng wooz

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Kxng Wooz is a Halifax-based Canadian hip-hop artist whose music pairs introspective lyricism with contemporary rap production. A longtime part of Nova Scotia's independent scene, he has released projects including Entering The..., Redemption and MALIK and collaborated with artists such as Ghettosocks, Aquakultre, Timbuktu and Leila Dey.
