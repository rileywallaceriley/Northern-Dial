northsidebaby

High Level, Alberta, Canada

NorthSideBaby, born Dawson Pastion-Bear in High Level, Alberta, is an Indigenous Canadian rapper and producer from Dene Tha’ First Nation. After beginning as a teenage beatmaker, he moved into recording his own vocals and built a touring career that has taken him through communities and cities across Canada.
