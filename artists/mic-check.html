Mic Check

Ottawa, Ontario, Canada

Mic Check is an Ottawa-based French-language rapper who appeared with DL Incognito on 'Savoir Faire' from the 2002 album A Sample and a Drum Machine and later on 'Fall Back.' Northern Dial's Mic Check entry is tied directly to that DL Incognito catalogue, and artist profiles identify Mic Check as representing Ottawa, Canada.
