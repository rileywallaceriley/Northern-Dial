jaclyn gee

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Jaclyn Gee is a Vancouver hip-hop and R&B artist whose catalogue includes the 2015 album Gypsy Rose and collaborations with Canadian rappers including Snak the Ripper, Merkules and Eternia. Northern Dial carries Jaclyn Gee as part of British Columbia's independent hip-hop and soul scene.
