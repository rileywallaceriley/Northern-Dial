julian thomas

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Julian Thomas is a Toronto rapper raised in Etobicoke who has been writing and recording hip-hop since his youth. His work draws on the west-end Toronto environment he grew up in, and he later signed with Sony Music Entertainment Canada while developing a catalogue that blends introspective writing with contemporary rap. Northern Dial carries Julian Thomas as part of its Toronto hip-hop catalogue.
