wendy macisaac

Cape Breton, Nova Scotia, Canada

Cape Breton fiddler, pianist and step dancer Wendy MacIsaac is a Canadian traditional musician whose work carries forward Nova Scotia's Gaelic and Cape Breton music traditions.
