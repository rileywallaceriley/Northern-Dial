i.r.s.

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

I.R.S. is an alternate library credit for the Canadian Scarborough hip-hop trio IRS, made up of Korry Deez, Black Cat and producer T.R.A.C.K.S. The Monolith-affiliated group released the Juno-nominated Welcome to Planet IRS and also appeared on The Real Frequency's Toronto compilation The New North.
