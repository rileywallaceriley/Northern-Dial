stephen hero

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Stephen Hero is an East Coast Canadian rapper, songwriter and producer whose work combines alternative hip-hop with a raw working-class perspective. After beginning to record as a teenager, he developed a self-produced catalogue spanning rap, experimental music and live instrumentation, including releases such as Just Because and Let's Get It.
