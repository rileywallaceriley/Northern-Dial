inderpal moga

Canada, Canada

Canadian-Punjabi singer Inderpal Moga has become a prominent voice in contemporary Punjabi music through a run of melodic street records and collaborations with artists including Chani Nattan, AR Paisley, Dr Zeus and Jazzy B. His catalogue bridges Punjabi music with the sound and perspective of the Canadian diaspora.
