jahmar carter

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Jahmar Carter is a Toronto-based producer and artist also known as Daxz and Lil DarkLord. As Daxz, he became known for production work connected to Drake's Grammy-nominated Back to Back and has worked with artists including KILLY, The Weeknd and Travis Scott. His co-credit with Shog Stan and WondaGurl on the 2026 single Girlfriend Pioneer Prodigy Veteran is his Northern Dial library credit.
