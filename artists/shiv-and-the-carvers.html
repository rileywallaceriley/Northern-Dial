Shiv and the Carvers

Shiv and the Carvers are a Toronto punk band building toward their debut full-length, Always Watching, due October 29, 2026 through Twin Fang Records. Their recent material pushes beyond straight-ahead punk with layered guitars, harmonies and added instrumentation while keeping the band’s confrontational live energy intact.

New single “Screens” arrived with the album announcement after a busy 2026 that also included “Executionary,” UK dates and a Second Summer Festival appearance in Toronto.
