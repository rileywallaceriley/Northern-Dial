jaye woods

Hamilton, Ontario, Canada

Jaye Woods is the stage name of Mississauga-raised, Hamilton-based hip-hop artist, producer and therapist Jordan Heywood. Active since 2011 and formerly recording as Orijin, he blends rap, melody and reflective writing around healing, sensitivity and community, with provincial tours and a Canadian Music Week appearance among his credits.
