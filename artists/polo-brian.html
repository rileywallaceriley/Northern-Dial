polo brian

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Polo Brian is a Vancouver-based British Columbia hip-hop artist whose off-kilter rap style has connected him with the West Coast independent scene. He reached a wider Canadian audience through Snotty Nose Rez Kids' 2021 single Wild Boy and later reunited with the duo on KIDS COME UP, IT'S SUPPERTIME! from I'M GOOD, HBU?. Those two collaborations are his Northern Dial library credits.
