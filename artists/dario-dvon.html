dario dvon

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Dario Dvon is a Barbadian-Canadian R&B and soul vocalist, songwriter, vocal coach and community arts facilitator based in Toronto. Originally from Barbados, he has become active in Toronto's live music scene through performances at Pride Toronto, The Drake Underground and other city venues while blending R&B, pop, soul and Caribbean influences. His feature on Sam-r's 2023 track Leap is his Northern Dial library credit.
