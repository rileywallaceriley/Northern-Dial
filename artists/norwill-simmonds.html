norwill simmonds

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Norwill Simmonds is a Jamaican-born, Canada-based gospel singer, songwriter and broadcaster who has spent decades making music in Ontario. After beginning to record as a child in Jamaica, he settled in Canada, recorded three gospel albums here and became host of Morning Dew Gospel Music on iLive Toronto. He is also the father of Daniel Caesar; his vocal and songwriting feature on Caesar's Baby Blue is the Northern Dial library credit.
