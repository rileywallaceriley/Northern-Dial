mayone9

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

MayOne-9 is a Canadian hip-hop artist associated with Toronto rap group BrassMunk and with producer Dub J's early-2000s Canadian hip-hop network. His work sits within the same era that connected artists such as JD Era, Eternia, D-Sisive and Moka Only. Northern Dial carries MayOne-9 as part of Canada's 2000s underground rap history.
