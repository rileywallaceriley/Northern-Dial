markus aurelyus

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

Markus Aurelyus is a Scarborough-based Trinidadian-Canadian singer-songwriter whose music blends R&B, soul, gospel, pop and hip-hop influences. His work reflects both his Caribbean heritage and Toronto-area upbringing. Northern Dial carries Markus Aurelyus as part of its contemporary Canadian R&B and soul catalogue.
