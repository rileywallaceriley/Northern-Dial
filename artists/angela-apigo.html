angela apigo

Ontario, Canada

Angela Apigo is an Ontario vocalist and songwriter active in Canadian R&B, soul and hip-hop collaborations. Her recorded work includes featured vocals on Big Jacks' Sunflower EP and Kutcorners' Round & Round, with credits connected to Toronto's independent soul and beat scene. Northern Dial carries Angela Apigo as part of its Canadian R&B and collaborator catalogue.
