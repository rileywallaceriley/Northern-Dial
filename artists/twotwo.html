twotwo

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

TWOTWO is a Toronto rapper associated with More Real Entertainment and the city's street-rap scene. He broke through alongside Pengz on the 2017 Toronto hit Griselda Blanco before releasing solo tracks including Ball and Wrist and later reuniting with Pengz for Gangsta. Griselda Blanco is his Northern Dial library credit.
