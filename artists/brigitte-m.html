brigitte m

Quebec, Canada

Brigitte M is a Quebec singer and stage performer whose career spans pop, musical theatre and orchestral reinterpretations of rock songs. Known professionally as Brigitte M and born Brigitte Marchand, she has appeared in major Quebec productions including Grease and released solo albums such as Toute moi and From Metal to Crystal. Northern Dial carries Brigitte M as part of its francophone Canadian vocal catalogue.
