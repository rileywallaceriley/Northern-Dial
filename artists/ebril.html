ebril

Hamilton, Ontario, Canada

Ebril is Iraqi-Canadian singer, songwriter and producer Huda Al-Hamami, an Ontario-based alternative artist whose self-produced debut album In Copula blends folk, lo-fi, shoegaze and field recordings. After her song Stranger in You became a viral breakthrough, she signed with Universal Music Canada and continued expanding her audience through Canadian festivals and touring.
