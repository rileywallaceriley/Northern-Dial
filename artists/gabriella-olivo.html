gabriella olivo

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Quebec City-born, Montreal-based singer-songwriter Gabriella Olivo blends her Mexican and Québécois roots through French- and Spanish-language Latin electro-pop. Drawing on bolero, cumbia, indie music and cinematic textures, she has earned recognition including a 2025-26 Radio-Canada Révélation nod and ADISQ and GAMIQ nominations for her bilingual work.
