lais

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Lais is a Toronto-based R&B singer and rapper whose moody independent catalogue drew early attention from OVO Sound Radio. Releases including SESSION ONE, Give Me One More and the 2018 single Once account for his four Northern Dial library credits.
