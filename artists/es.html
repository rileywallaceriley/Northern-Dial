es

Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

Es, born Eseme Elango, is a Mississauga hip-hop MC whose work is rooted in golden-era boom-bap and the Gillbreathing family alongside Elaquent, Solar-C and Mathematik. He has released a long-running independent catalogue through projects including Feud For Thought, We Are Only Getting Older and You Want A Piece Of Me?. His appearance on Elaquent's Carnival with Mathematik and Solar-C is his Northern Dial library credit.
