theo day

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Theo Day is a Canadian indie-folk and Americana singer-songwriter emerging from Montreal's independent music scene. Since debuting in 2025, his acoustic storytelling and folk-pop sound have powered releases including the Homegrown EP, sold-out Montreal shows and festival appearances including LASSO Montréal.
