simar

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto-born and raised singer, songwriter and composer Simar is a Canadian Punjabi artist blending hip-hop, R&B and pop with Punjabi-language songwriting. She has collaborated with artists including Sidhu Moosewala, Fateh and Talwiinder while building a bilingual catalogue that places a female perspective at the centre of contemporary Punjabi music.
