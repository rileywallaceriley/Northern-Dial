nana quinn

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Montreal-based Nana Quinn is a multidisciplinary Quebec artist, writer and musician whose work moves between poetry, performance, visual art and experimental folk-pop. A Radio-Canada poetry prize recipient and CALQ-supported artist, Quinn has released books alongside musical projects including tout me quitte / tout vient à moi.
