Northern Dial Artist Profile

8485

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto artist 8485, often called Eighty, emerged from the internet-native experimental pop community at the turn of the 2020s, bringing together hyperpop's digital abrasion with a more melodic, emotionally exposed songwriting style. Early releases including alpha testing and the 2021 EP plague town developed a cybernetic visual language around music that could jump between glitchy pop, emo, electronic production and heavier guitar-rooted instincts. The project grew alongside collaborators in the Helix Tears orbit, particularly producer blackwinterwells, while 8485's writing increasingly treated online identity as a framework for very human anxiety, attachment and self-scrutiny.

By 2023's Personal Protocol, 8485 had expanded that vocabulary into drum and bass, breakbeats and atmospheric dance-pop without losing the intimate writing at the centre of the project. The record was written and performed by 8485 and executive produced by blackwinterwells, with a wider group of underground producers contributing across its tracks. Her profile continued to widen through collaborations and live activity, including the 2025 single “G.I.R.L.” with Danny Brown and a guest appearance on Brown's Stardust, connecting a scene born largely through online networks to an established figure in experimental hip-hop.

8485's Canadian significance is less about conventional chart crossover than about representing a generation of Toronto music that developed simultaneously online and locally. Exclaim! has described her as a Toronto scene mainstay, while The FADER profiled her early as part of a digitally connected underground-pop movement. Her work shows how Canadian independent pop in the 2020s can build community, aesthetics and international reach without passing through the older radio-and-label pipeline first.
