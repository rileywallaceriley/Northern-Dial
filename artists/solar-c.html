solar-c

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Solar-C is a Canadian hip-hop lyricist associated with the Toronto-area Gillbreathing family alongside Elaquent, Es and Mathematik. His catalogue includes the 2018 album Access To All Lanes, the CrossLakes collaboration Sunshowers and later work with Reazhun, Mathematik and Lanai. His appearance with Mathematik and Es on Elaquent's Carnival is the Northern Dial library credit.
