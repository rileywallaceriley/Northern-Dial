bebeboy

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Bebeboy is a Toronto-based R&B and hip-hop artist, songwriter and producer whose melodic catalogue blends contemporary R&B, pop and rap. Born outside Canada and raised within Toronto's music scene, Bebeboy has built an independent catalogue of emotionally direct songs and collaborations. Northern Dial carries Bebeboy as part of its contemporary Toronto R&B catalogue.
