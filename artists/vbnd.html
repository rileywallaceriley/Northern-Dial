vbnd

Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada

vbnd is a Saskatoon, Saskatchewan bassist, producer and recording artist whose music blends jazz, funk, neo-soul and alternative R&B. Signed to UK label DeepMatter, he built his catalogue through releases including Daughter of the Sun and Scum Funk, often working with the Saskatoon musicians who became The Soulmate Collective. Ten vbnd tracks from those projects and related singles appear in the Northern Dial library.
