2 versatile

Canada, Canada

2 Versatile is a Canadian old-school hip-hop act whose self-titled album appeared in 1994. The group is part of the early Canadian rap catalogue and is associated with recordings including Cuddle with Me, which features Canadian R&B singer Glenn Lewis. Northern Dial carries 2 Versatile as part of that 1990s Canadian hip-hop lineage.
