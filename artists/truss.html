truss

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Brampton-raised, Toronto-based Truss is a Canadian singer, rapper, songwriter, producer and audio engineer whose solo work and duo TRP.P blend hip-hop, R&B and soul. A self-taught multi-instrumentalist, he has composed for Canadian television, toured nationally and internationally and released solo projects including All Day Breakfast, Cavity, Root Canal and All We Ask Is Truss.
