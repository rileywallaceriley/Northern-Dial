chasé scanz

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Chasé Scanz is a Canadian hip-hop artist associated with Vancouver's Indigenous rap scene and the Hereditary Sound circle. His collaborations with Sekawnee and EfrainYB include Nations, Relatives and Get Up and Dance, while his own releases include Fashion District. Northern Dial carries Chasé Scanz as part of its contemporary Indigenous and West Coast Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
