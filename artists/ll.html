Northern Dial Artist Profile

LØLØ

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

LØLØ is a Toronto-born singer-songwriter who turns the stuff people usually keep in drafts, notes apps and unsent texts into sharp pop-rock songs. Since beginning her career in 2018, she has built a catalogue around hyper-specific confession, guitar-driven hooks and a mix of nineties alternative attitude and modern pop immediacy.

Her 2024 debut album, falling for robots & wishing i was one, pushed that voice into a full-length format and helped expand an audience already growing through earlier EPs and online releases. Touring with artists including jxdn, Boys Like Girls, Simple Plan and Against The Current, plus appearances at festivals including Warped Tour, Download, Slam Dunk, Rock am Ring and Lollapalooza, moved the project well beyond the internet without sanding down the writing that made it connect in the first place.

In 2026, LØLØ made her Fearless Records debut with god forbid a girl spits out her feelings!, a 13-track second album built around a more stripped-back, diaristic approach. Songs including “007,” “delusional darling,” “lobotomy & u” and “the punisher” lean into the uncomfortable details of relationships, jealousy, intrusive thoughts and growing up. Northern Dial's feature on “the punisher” digs into one of the clearest examples: a song born from knowing that checking an ex's new life online will hurt and doing it anyway.

That specificity is the through-line. LØLØ can make a chorus big enough for a festival crowd without losing the feeling that the lyric started as something she probably was not supposed to say out loud.
