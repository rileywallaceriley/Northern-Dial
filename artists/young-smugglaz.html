Young Smugglaz

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Young Smugglaz is a Toronto-area hip-hop artist connected to the Jane and Finch rap scene. The artist appears on the 2024 project 'Jane & Finch Most Wanted, Vol. 1,' including 'Sam Taylor' with Young Don Million and 'RIP JR. D.' Northern Dial carries Young Smugglaz as part of its Jane and Finch and Toronto hip-hop catalogue.
