Diamond

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Diamond is the Northern Dial catalogue name for Toronto underground rapper Diamond X, whose 'On My Own' appears on the 1998 local compilation Invading the Mega City. Northern Dial also credits Diamond on 'Murda Style' alongside Madlocks and Infinite. The matching release credit identifies this catalogue entry with Toronto's late-1990s independent hip-hop scene.
