the blk lt$

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

The BLK LT$ is the musical project of Toronto-born singer, songwriter and producer Andreena Miller. Known earlier as Andreena Mill, she has earned Juno nominations, collaborated with artists and producers across hip-hop and R&B and developed a catalogue that moves between soul, R&B and hip-hop production.
