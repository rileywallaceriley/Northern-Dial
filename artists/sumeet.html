sumeet

London, Ontario, Canada

Sumeet is a Canadian singer-songwriter born and raised in London, Ontario whose mid-2000s music blended hip-hop, R&B, dancehall and South Asian influences. Her independent breakthrough included the Elephant Man-assisted single Agony and the album Deeper, with her work receiving Canadian radio play and collaborations across Toronto's urban music scene.
