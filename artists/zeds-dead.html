zeds dead

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto electronic duo Zeds Dead, formed by Dylan Mamid and Zachary Rapp-Rovan, has built an international bass-music career around dubstep, drum and bass, house, hip-hop sampling and the Deadbeats label.
