sargeant

Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Sargeant is Calgary vocalist Yolanda Sargeant, one half of the acclaimed lo-fi soul duo Sargeant X Comrade. Her expressive vocals sit at the centre of the group's blend of soul, hip-hop, funk and sample-driven production. Northern Dial carries Sargeant as part of its Calgary and Western Canadian soul catalogue.
