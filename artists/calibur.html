Calibur

Ajax, Ontario, Canada

Calibur is the hip-hop stage name used by Ajax vocalist Najja Crest, who emerged from Durham Region's early-2000s music scene after performing with the groups Foot Soldiers and Bush Life. A contemporary Pickering-area profile identifies Najja 'Calibur' Crest as an Ajax artist who had opened for acts including Boot Camp, Jeru and Ghetto Concept. Northern Dial carries Calibur as part of its Durham Region and Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
