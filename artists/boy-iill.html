boy-iill

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Halifax MC Boy-Ill, born Matt Boyle, came up in Nova Scotia's hip-hop community as a member of Fax4 with Spesh K, Quake Matthews and Markit. He later partnered with longtime friend Markit in Something Good, developing their two-MC setup into a live-band project that toured beyond Halifax.
