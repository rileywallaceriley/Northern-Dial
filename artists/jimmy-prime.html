jimmy prime

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Jimmy Prime is a Toronto rapper and member of the Prime Boys who became closely identified with the city's 2010s hip-hop rise. Raised in Toronto and embedded in the Prime collective, he is widely credited with helping popularize The 6 as a nickname for the city. His appearance alongside SAFE on Smoke Dawg's YND is his Northern Dial library credit.
