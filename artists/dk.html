dk

Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, Canada

DK is producer Darrell Kelloway, a Newfoundland-raised, Nova Scotia-based beatmaker known for sample-rich boom-bap production. Based in the Halifax-Dartmouth area, he has worked closely with Canadian MCs including Ghettosocks and has released projects such as Soul Expressions, Listen to the Masters and Atlantic Rap Vol. 1. Northern Dial carries DK as part of its Canadian hip-hop producer catalogue.
