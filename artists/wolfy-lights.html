wolfy lights

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Wolfy Lights is the electronic and drum-and-bass project of Sherry St. Germain, a classically trained multi-instrumentalist, producer, singer and songwriter with Toronto roots. St. Germain has also worked under the AKYLLA name and has collaborated with artists across electronic, hip-hop, film and television music. Bombalaya, its Blooom remix and Born Again are the three Wolfy Lights tracks in the Northern Dial library.
