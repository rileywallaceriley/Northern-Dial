francesco antonio

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Francesco Antonio is a Toronto-born actor, recording artist, writer and director whose music career developed alongside his film and television work. After meeting Canadian reggae artist Snow in a Toronto studio, he joined Snow's DKO Music Group and became part of the label's emerging artist roster. His appearance on Snow's 2020 single Together with William Dinero and Balam Kiel is his Northern Dial library credit.
