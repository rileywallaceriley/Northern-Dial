Tassja

Canada

Tassja is a Canadian singer associated with Toronto and Halifax hip-hop and R&B circles. Her catalogue includes the 2006 Spesh K collaboration 'Retirement Days,' while she later appeared in Drake's 'Started From the Bottom' video and pursued her own music as Tassja Dawn. Northern Dial carries Tassja as part of its Canadian R&B and hip-hop catalogue.
