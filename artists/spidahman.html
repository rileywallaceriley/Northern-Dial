spidahman

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

Scarborough rapper Spidahman is a veteran member of Toronto’s Monolith collective whose forceful, battle-ready delivery appears across the crew’s late-1990s and 2000s catalogue. His credits include Monolith’s The Long Awaited..., Dan-e-o records such as Raw Is War and The Real Deal, and Wio-K’s posse cut Ten.
