jrdn

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Halifax-born JRDN, born Ralph Jordon Croucher, is a Canadian R&B singer whose career grew from Nova Scotia's music scene into a Juno-recognized blend of R&B, soul and hip-hop.
