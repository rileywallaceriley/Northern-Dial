x2fromtheciv

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

X2FromTheCIV is a hip-hop artist active in Toronto's independent music community. His catalogue includes State of Mind and Heavily Humbled, along with recurring collaborations with Toronto-based artist and engineer Sam-r, HXGO, Tadiie and other members of the surrounding scene. Northern Dial carries X2FromTheCIV as part of its contemporary Toronto hip-hop catalogue.
