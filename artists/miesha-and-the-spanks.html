Miesha & The Spanks

Miesha & The Spanks are a Calgary garage-punk duo led by singer-songwriter and guitarist Miesha Louie with drummer Sean Hamilton. Their live sound leans on fuzzy guitar, hard-driving drums and a sharp pop instinct beneath the noise.

The pair return September 18, 2026 with the Visions EP, produced by Juno Award winner Hill Kourkoutis. The release marks a new chapter after their 2023 album Unconditional Love in Hi-Fi and arrives with hometown release shows in Calgary.
