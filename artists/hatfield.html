hatfield

Weymouth, Nova Scotia, Canada

Hatfield is a Canadian rapper and singer-songwriter from Weymouth, Nova Scotia, formerly known as Khaki Ka$h and Just Q. His music moves between golden-era boom bap, melodic rap and alternative influences, reflecting a long-running connection to Nova Scotia's independent hip-hop community. Northern Dial carries Hatfield as part of its Atlantic Canadian rap catalogue.
