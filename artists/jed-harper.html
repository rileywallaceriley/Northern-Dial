jed harper

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Jed Harper is a Newmarket-born, Toronto-based DJ and producer whose career spans hip-hop, house and club music. Formerly known as DJ Jedi, he became a fixture in Toronto nightlife, served as the Toronto Blue Jays' official DJ and performed at major festivals and clubs in Canada and abroad. Northern Dial carries Jed Harper as part of its Canadian DJ and electronic music catalogue.
