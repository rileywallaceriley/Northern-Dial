pino

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Ajax-born, Toronto-based singer-songwriter and producer Pino is part of a new generation of Canadian R&B artists reviving the warmth of early-2000s soul and R&B through modern production. His 2025 EP '02 features Toronto collaborators including Dylan Sinclair, Avenoir, Maz B and Norah's World and reflects the community-oriented approach of the city's emerging R&B scene.
