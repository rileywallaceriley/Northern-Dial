haliey smith

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Halifax singer-songwriter Haliey Smith is an African Nova Scotian R&B and soul artist with deep roots in the province's music community. A longtime session and background vocalist, she has performed with artists including David Myles and Joel Plaskett, appeared with Symphony Nova Scotia and stepped into a solo career with releases including Fall In Love.
