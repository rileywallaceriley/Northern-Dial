Housewife

Housewife is the Toronto indie-pop project of Brighid Fry. Fry first emerged as one half of Moscow Apartment before continuing under the Housewife name, developing a more personal strain of guitar-driven pop with room for genre shifts and sharper songwriting.

After the 2025 EP Girl of the Hour, Housewife returned in March 2026 with “Misbehavin’,” extending a run that has steadily pushed the project beyond its early indie-rock roots.
