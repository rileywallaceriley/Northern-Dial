runway the catwalker

Ajax, Ontario, Canada

Runway the Catwalker is an Ajax, Ontario artist, songwriter and producer based in Toronto whose genre-bending work moves through rap, punk, indie and alternative music. Alongside his own releases, he has built an international songwriting and production résumé that includes work connected to Doja Cat, A$AP Rocky, Yxng Bane and D-Block Europe.
