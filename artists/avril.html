avril

Napanee, Ontario, Canada

Avril is a split library credit for Canadian singer-songwriter Avril Lavigne on the 2020 ArtistsCAN charity recording of Lean on Me. The Napanee-raised pop-rock star joined more than two dozen Canadian artists for the Sony Music Canada release supporting Canadian Red Cross COVID-19 relief.
