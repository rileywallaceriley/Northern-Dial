EfrainYB

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

EfrainYB is an Indigenous hip-hop artist active in British Columbia's contemporary rap scene. The artist records frequently with Sekawnee, including 'Nations,' 'Relatives' and 'BOI,' and has appeared with that circle in Vancouver live listings. Northern Dial carries EfrainYB as part of its emerging West Coast Indigenous hip-hop catalogue.
