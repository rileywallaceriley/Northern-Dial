OLIVER

Vancouver / Los Angeles, Canada

OLIVER is the electronic production duo of Vaughn Oliver and Oliver Goldstein. Northern Dial carries the duo's 2017 track 'Go With It' from the album Full Circle. Vaughn Oliver is a Canadian producer originally associated with Vancouver, and the duo's catalogue blends electro, nu-disco and pop production. Northern Dial retains OLIVER in its Canadian catalogue based on Vaughn Oliver's Canadian identity and the exact Full Circle recording credit.
