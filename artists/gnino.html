gnino

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Gnino is a Montreal rapper from Tétreaultville in the city's east end who grew up immersed in Quebec hip-hop through his father, rapper Rjboy. After beginning to write in his teens, he built momentum through the Cagoulé Gang duo and solo releases including Paranoia, developing a melodic, emotionally direct style within Montreal's francophone rap scene.
