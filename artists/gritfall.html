gritfall

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Gritfall is a Toronto hip-hop artist associated with the city's current underground rap scene. The catalogue includes solo material and collaborations with artists including Shadee, Forest N/A and Rico Blox. Northern Dial carries Gritfall as part of its emerging Canadian hip-hop coverage.
