meeno giinchy

Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

Meeno Giinchy is a Mississauga hip-hop artist and collaborator active in the Greater Toronto Area rap scene. His catalogue includes solo releases and collaborations with artists from Toronto and surrounding communities, placing him within Southern Ontario's independent hip-hop network. Northern Dial carries Meeno Giinchy as part of its GTA hip-hop catalogue.
