maria-therese

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto-born R&B and soul singer-songwriter Maria-Therese is a Canadian artist whose work pairs intimate storytelling with themes of resilience, healing and personal growth. Her releases have reached CBC, BBC Radio, SiriusXM and major streaming platforms, while the Warner Music Canada-released EP MONARCH marked a major step in her contemporary R&B career.
