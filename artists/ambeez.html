ambeez

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Ambeez is an alternate recording name used by Halifax rapper and producer Ambition, a longtime member of Nova Scotia's independent hip-hop community and the wider Backburner collective. His catalogue includes solo work, collaborations with Uncle Fester and later releases as one half of The Library Steps with Jesse Dangerously.
