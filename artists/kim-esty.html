kim esty

Canada

Canadian Eurodance singer and producer Kim Esty built a run of dance releases in the 1990s before reaching a wider audience as the featured vocalist on The Boomtang Boys’ hit singles Squeeze Toy and Pictures. Squeeze Toy topped the Canadian singles chart and won Best Dance Video at the 1999 MuchMusic Video Awards.
