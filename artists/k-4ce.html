k-4ce

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Pioneering Toronto MC Shawn 'K-4ce' Morrison is an early architect of the city's hip-hop identity, remembered for coining the term 'T-Dot' and for work with artists including Michie Mee, Maestro Fresh Wes and Ghetto Concept. Raised between Toronto's Weston neighbourhood and New York City, he helped shape the vocabulary and culture of Toronto rap before the city's later international breakthrough.
