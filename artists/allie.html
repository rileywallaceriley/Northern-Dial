allie

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Allie is the library credit for Toronto R&B singer-songwriter Allison Ho-Sang, who records as a l l i e. Her atmospheric blend of soul, electronic music and pop has earned SOCAN Songwriting Prize recognition, a Polaris Music Prize longlist and a JUNO nomination, while collaborations include Illa J and Canadian production duo Potatohead People on Sunflower.
