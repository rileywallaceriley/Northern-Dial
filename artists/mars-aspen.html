Mars Aspen

Mars Aspen is a Canadian alternative artist signed to Twin Fang Records. Their baby teeth EP is built around snapshots of past relationships, mental-health struggles and the process of moving toward a more purposeful life.

The project moves through songs including “spilled milk,” “i’d like to stay mad,” “pest control” and “baby teeth,” framing personal growth without sanding down the difficult parts.
