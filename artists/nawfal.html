nawfal

Montreal, Quebec, Canada

Nawfal is a Montreal rapper whose work sits in contemporary francophone hip-hop, often drawing on Maghrebi identity and local Quebec culture. His releases include SECTEUR NORD, L.X.I.V and the 2025 project blues beldi with producer Kheir; their track archive 2001 is the Northern Dial library entry.
