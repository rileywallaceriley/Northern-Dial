soft eyez

London, Ontario, Canada

Soft Eyez is a producer and recording artist associated with London, Ontario hip-hop collective 4DaTribe. He has released solo instrumental work and built an extensive collaborative catalogue with KOTO?, including Mornington Me and the 4DaTribe Disc projects. DerekFisher, recorded with KOTO? and chai sully, is his Northern Dial library credit.
