only1kng

Canada, Canada

Only1KNG is a Canadian producer and musician whose work is closely connected to Toronto's independent hip-hop and R&B community. He has produced and performed with artists including Junia-T, Tea Fannie, STORRY, Samalong and Borelson, contributing keyboards, production and collaborative releases across Canadian projects. Northern Dial carries Only1KNG as part of its contemporary Canadian producer catalogue.
