Lokz

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Lokz is the former stage name of Toronto singer, songwriter and producer Yuri Koller. Koller built his early career in Toronto under the Lokz name, appearing on JD Era's 'No Handouts,' performing at Toronto showcases and earning a slot at the first OVO Fest before later recording under his birth name. Northern Dial carries Lokz as part of its Toronto hip-hop and R&B catalogue.
