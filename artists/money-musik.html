money musik

Brampton, Ontario, Canada

Money Musik, born Mohkom Singh Bhangal, is a Brampton, Ontario producer whose early work with Toronto artists including Pressa, WhyG, Casper TNG and LB Spiffy grew into an international production career. He became a key collaborator of NAV and produced the full AP Dhillon and Gurinder Gill album Not by Chance. Northern Dial carries one of the AP Dhillon, Gurinder Gill and Money Musik recordings from that era.
