dominique way

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Barrie-born, Toronto-based singer-songwriter Dominique Way is a Canadian indie-pop artist known for warm melodies, intimate storytelling and early-2000s pop influences. After studying music industry arts at Fanshawe College, she built momentum with releases including Sprig and Who Could Not Be Broken and signed with Cult Nation Records.
