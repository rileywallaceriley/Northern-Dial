Toronto, Ontario · Psychedelic / Garage Rock

Pretty

Pretty are a Toronto psychedelic garage rock band built around Torin Craig, Eliot Rossi, Will Macquarrie and Morgan Zych. Their sound pulls from garage rock, psych-pop and acid-rock textures without losing the scrappy feel of a club band.

After releasing Citrus Magic, the band entered a busy 2026 stretch with “Burning Money,” “Bored,” “Great Work,” “Ziggurats” and “Same Old,” setting up a new album cycle and renewed momentum.
