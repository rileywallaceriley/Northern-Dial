caleb awiti

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Caleb Awiti is a Kenyan-born, Vancouver-based R&B artist whose music blends contemporary soul, pop and melodic hip-hop influences. His independent catalogue includes releases such as SBLTLA, HYPNOTIC and I Can't Change Time, and his work has been featured in Canadian discovery playlists including Touchpoints: Vancouver. Northern Dial carries Caleb Awiti as part of its contemporary West Coast R&B catalogue.
