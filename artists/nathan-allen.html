nathan allen

Brampton, Ontario, Canada

Nathan Allen is a contemporary jazz saxophonist from Brampton, Ontario whose sound blends smooth jazz, R&B and hip-hop influences. He studied at York University in Toronto and built a large online audience through saxophone performances and covers, while continuing to perform regularly across the Greater Toronto Area. Northern Dial carries Nathan Allen as part of its contemporary Canadian jazz and instrumental catalogue.
