kai banks

London, Ontario, Canada

Kai Banks is a rapper and recording artist in London, Ontario's 4DaTribe collective, part of a new generation of Canadian hip-hop artists emerging from the city. His solo catalogue includes The Kaible and 08 JETTA, while Too Deep For The Outro with La Reezy and Mileena is his Northern Dial library credit.
