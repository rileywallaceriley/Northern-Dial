Winnipeg, Manitoba · Dream Pop / Slowcore

Living Hour

Living Hour are a Winnipeg band whose music drifts between dreamy noise rock, slowcore, indie folk and fuzzy pop. Sam Sarty’s writing gives the band’s hazy arrangements a grounded centre, often turning small details and passing moments into the emotional core of a song.

The band returns September 24, 2026 with the Magnet EP, following 2025’s Internal Drone Infinity. The release lands alongside Canadian dates in Ottawa, Montreal and Toronto.
