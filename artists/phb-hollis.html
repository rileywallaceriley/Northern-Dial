PHB Hollis

Brampton, Ontario, Canada

PHB Hollis is a Canadian hip-hop artist connected to Brampton-rooted Capital Vision and the wider Toronto-area rap scene. His catalogue includes singles such as 'Switched Up' and 'Same Thing,' along with Capital Vision collaborations including 'Lost Time,' 'Pray For Me,' 'Wild Life' and 'Soul Complete.' Northern Dial carries PHB Hollis as part of its emerging GTA hip-hop catalogue.
