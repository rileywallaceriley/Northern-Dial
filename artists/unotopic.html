unotopic

Edmonton, Alberta, Canada

Unotopic is an Edmonton artist with Canadian and Guatemalan roots whose music sits within the urbano latino space. Born in 2001 and based in Alberta, he blends Latin urban influences with contemporary pop and hip-hop production. Northern Dial carries Unotopic as part of its Western Canadian Latin and urban catalogue.
