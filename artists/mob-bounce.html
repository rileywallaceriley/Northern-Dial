mob bounce

Northern British Columbia, Canada

Northern British Columbia duo Mob Bounce is an Indigenous Canadian hip-hop group formed by Gitxsan artist The Northwest Kid and Cree-Métis artist EarthChild, blending electronic beats with culture, healing and social justice.
