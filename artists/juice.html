juice

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Juice is a Toronto hip-hop artist also known as Rochester or Rochester aka Juice. A longtime figure in the city's rap scene, he has released solo work under both names and has been documented by Canadian music press as a Toronto MC. Northern Dial carries Juice as part of its Toronto hip-hop catalogue.
