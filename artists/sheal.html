sheal

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Halifax-born, Toronto-based indie-pop singer-songwriter SHEAL, the stage name of Sheilagh McNab, makes piano-led songs built around intimate writing and emotive vocals. Her releases include the golden hours EP and the home-going project, while her music has received national CBC radio play and collaborations with Canadian artists including Eternia, Rel McCoy and Shad.
