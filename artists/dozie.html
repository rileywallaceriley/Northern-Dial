dozie

Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Dozie is a Calgary-based hip-hop and afro-pop artist whose music blends rap with melodic and Afrobeats influences. His work has received Canadian playlist and radio support, and he has toured in Canada and Europe while building a growing catalogue of independent releases. Northern Dial carries Dozie as part of its contemporary Western Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
