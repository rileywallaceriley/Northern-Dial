rach

Laval, Quebec, Canada

Rach is a Quebec rapper and former beatmaker from Laval-des-Rapides. He emerged through tracks such as Chief Keef, Sans refrain, FNG and collaborations with J.BO, JPS and Tizzo, building a catalogue rooted in Laval's francophone street-rap scene. His feature on JMB Drippy's 2021 single Palmarès is the RACH credit in the Northern Dial library.
