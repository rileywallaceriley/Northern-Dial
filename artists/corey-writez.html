Corey Writez

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Corey Writez is a Halifax hip-hop artist active in Nova Scotia's independent rap scene. He appears on Aquakultre and DJ Uncle Fester's Halifax-recorded album Bleeding Gums Murphy on the track 'Autographs' and has been billed as a local Halifax act in live-event promotion. Northern Dial carries Corey Writez as part of its Atlantic Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
