arcee

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto MC and radio host Arcee is a veteran of Canada's independent hip-hop scene, known for sharp battle-oriented lyricism and for co-hosting the influential Real Frequency show on CKLN. His early singles and collaborations with producers including Da Grassroots, DJ Serious and Jake One were collected on the MixedWell mixtape, while his catalogue also includes appearances alongside Brassmunk, IRS, King Reign and other Toronto artists.
