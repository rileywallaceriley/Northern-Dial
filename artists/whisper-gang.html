Whisper Gang

Whisper Gang are a Toronto four-piece whose music folds shoegaze, grunge, dream pop and space-rock into a hazy alternative sound. The band is made up of Day Jasper, Nick Mokrzewski, Bailey Skilton and Sean Winick.

After the Becoming Pt. 1 EP, they kept building through 2025 and 2026 with “Answers,” “Bend” and “Event Horizon,” alongside shows across Ontario and Quebec.
