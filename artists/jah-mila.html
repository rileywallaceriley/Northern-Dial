jah'mila

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Jamaican-born Canadian reggae singer Jah'Mila is a Halifax-based artist whose roots reggae incorporates soul and jazz. Her debut album Roots Girl won East Coast recognition and earned a Juno nomination, and her career has included performances and collaborations with Symphony Nova Scotia, the National Arts Centre Orchestra and the Toronto Symphony Orchestra.
