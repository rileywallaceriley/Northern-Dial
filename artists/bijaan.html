bijaan

Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Bijaan is a Calgary-born and based MC whose music blends old-school hip-hop foundations with a global perspective shaped by family roots in East Africa and India. He has performed at events including NXNE, Sled Island and JUNO Week, and his work has earned attention as part of a rising generation of Canadian hip-hop. Northern Dial carries Bijaan as part of its Western Canadian rap catalogue.
