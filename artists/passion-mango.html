passion mango

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Passion Mango is a Vancouver indie and bedroom-pop project led by songwriter and musician Connor Wilkinson. The project's dreamy sound combines indie pop, lo-fi textures and melodic songwriting. Northern Dial carries Passion Mango as part of its contemporary British Columbia independent music catalogue.
