ironside

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Ironside is a Toronto hip-hop artist associated with Ghetto Concept and the 7Bills scene. He appeared alongside major Canadian rappers on Ghetto Concept's Still Too Much and is part of the documented network around Toronto's late-1990s and early-2000s underground rap movement. Northern Dial carries Ironside as part of its archival Toronto hip-hop catalogue.
