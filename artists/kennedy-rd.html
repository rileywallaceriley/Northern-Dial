kennedy rd.

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto native Kennedy Rd. is an R&B singer-songwriter whose atmospheric, emotionally direct sound draws on 1990s and early-2000s R&B influences. Her catalogue includes the Feelings Cafe EP and collaborations across Toronto's contemporary R&B scene.
