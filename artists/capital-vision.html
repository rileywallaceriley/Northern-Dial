Capital Vision

Brampton, Ontario, Canada

Capital Vision is a Brampton-rooted Canadian urban music collective and management project founded in 2024. Its releases and artist network connect a group of emerging Peel Region hip-hop acts, including JayTrillz, PHB Hollis, Froboii and Punchinrd. Northern Dial carries Capital Vision as part of its contemporary Brampton and GTA hip-hop catalogue.
