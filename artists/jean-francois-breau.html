jean françois breau

Tracadie-Sheila, New Brunswick, Canada

Acadian singer-songwriter Jean-François Breau was born in Ontario and raised in Tracadie-Sheila, New Brunswick, building a francophone career through solo records, musical theatre and the Canadian group Salebarbes.
