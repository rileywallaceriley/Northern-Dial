latoya

Ontario, Canada

Latoya is a Canadian R&B vocalist associated with producer Dub J's Kitchener-Waterloo and Toronto-area hip-hop network. Dub J identifies Latoya among the Canadian talent he produced during the growth of his Q It Up Records catalogue. Northern Dial carries Latoya as part of the Canadian R&B and hip-hop ecosystem surrounding that era.
