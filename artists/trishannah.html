trishannah

Edmonton, Alberta, Canada

Trishannah is an Edmonton-based vocalist and multidisciplinary artist whose work spans solo performance, community arts and collaborative music projects. She has performed under the Trishannah Gentles Music name and has participated in Edmonton arts initiatives including 5 Artists One Love and CASA choir projects. Northern Dial carries Trishannah as part of its Alberta vocal and independent music catalogue.
