maddie bay

Montréal, Quebec, Canada

Maddie Bay is a France-born, Montréal-based singer-songwriter, vocalist and producer whose alternative pop-folk blends delicate acoustic writing with electronic textures. Her professional bio identifies Maddie Bay as her artist project and notes that she has been living in Canada for several years. Her 2026 single fin pm is her Northern Dial library credit.
