cola h.

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Cola H., also known as Colanthony Humphrey, is a Toronto multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, producer and artist active across the city's rock, hip-hop and independent music communities. He is also known for his work with The OBGMs and for production and collaborative projects beyond the band. Northern Dial carries Cola H. as part of its Toronto artist and producer catalogue.
