king dapz

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

King Dapz is a Scarborough rapper associated with Toronto's independent hip-hop scene. He has released solo material and collaborated with fellow Scarborough artist Gangis Khan, with tracks including Drip, Done and Angels (Freestyle). Northern Dial carries King Dapz as part of its contemporary Toronto rap catalogue.
