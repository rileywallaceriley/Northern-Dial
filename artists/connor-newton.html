connor newton

Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada

Connor Newton is a Saskatoon-born Canadian saxophonist, multi-instrumentalist, producer and educator. After studying jazz performance in Edmonton and at the University of Toronto, he returned to Saskatoon and has worked with artists including Bombargo, vbnd, Katie Tupper and Collective Order while also releasing hip-hop-informed solo work as Contrafact. His saxophone performances on vbnd's Name (On My Tongue) and No Other Feeling account for his Northern Dial library credits.
