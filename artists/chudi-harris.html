chudi harris

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Halifax singer-songwriter and recording artist Chudi Harris is part of Nova Scotia's independent R&B and hip-hop community. His alternative R&B catalogue includes multiple EPs and collaborations, with performances and recordings connecting him to artists including Aquakultre, T. Thomason, Ghettosocks and Tachichi.
