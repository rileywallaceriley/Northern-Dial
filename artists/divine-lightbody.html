divine lightbody

Greater Toronto Area, Ontario, Canada

Divine Lightbody is a Greater Toronto Area-born Canadian R&B and soul singer, songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist. A finalist on CTV's The Launch, she has developed a Toronto-rooted catalogue blending modern R&B with jazz and gospel influence while collaborating with artists and producers including Xavier Omär and Matthew Burnett. Her 2025 track nightlife with Canadian producer savvan is her Northern Dial library credit.
