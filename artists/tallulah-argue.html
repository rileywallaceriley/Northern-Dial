tallulah argue

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Toronto-born singer-songwriter Tallulah Argue is a Canadian artist whose music combines country, alternative pop and grungy Americana with witty, narrative-driven writing. After beginning her career in Toronto, she relocated to London to study and develop her work, releasing songs including Moonshine and the 2026 EP Deer in the Headlights.
