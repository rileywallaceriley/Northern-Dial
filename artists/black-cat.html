black cat

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

Scarborough rapper and producer Black Cat is a member of the Canadian hip-hop group IRS and the larger Monolith collective. Alongside Korry Deez and producer T.R.A.C.K.S., he helped shape IRS’s Welcome to Planet IRS era and Toronto underground records connected to Monolith and Mastermind.
