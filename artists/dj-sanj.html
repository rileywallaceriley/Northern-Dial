dj sanj

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

DJ Sanj is a Canadian Punjabi and Bhangra producer whose career began in Toronto in the late 1990s before expanding internationally. Known for Desi mixtapes, Punjabi production and crossover work, he later became a prominent producer across the UK and South Asian music industries while maintaining strong Canadian ties. Northern Dial carries DJ Sanj as part of Canada's Punjabi music history.
