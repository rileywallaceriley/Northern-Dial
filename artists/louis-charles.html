louis-charles

Quebec City, Quebec, Canada

Louis-Charles is a Quebec City quintet whose music blends post-punk, post-rock and alternative pop with francophone songwriting. The group released the EP BABY in 2023 and followed it with the album Louis-Charles II in 2026, establishing a growing presence in Quebec's independent rock scene. Northern Dial carries Louis-Charles as part of its contemporary francophone alternative catalogue.
