pay$o

Canada, Canada

Pay$o is a Canadian hip-hop artist whose catalogue includes tracks such as Digital Minds, St-Antoine, Dollarama, Rudimentary and Game Changer. His music draws on contemporary urban rap while reflecting everyday life and personal experience, and he has appeared alongside other Montreal-linked independent artists. Northern Dial carries Pay$o as part of its contemporary Canadian hip-hop catalogue.
