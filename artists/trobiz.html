trobiz

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada

Halifax hip-hop artist, producer and entrepreneur Tremayne 'Trobiz' Howe has been part of Nova Scotia's rap community since the early 1990s. A member of the early group The Overlords, he later became known for production work with artists including JRDN and Classified while also operating Fademasters in Halifax's North End.
