charisma aka skizz

Scarborough, Ontario, Canada

Scarborough producer and MC Charisma, also known as Charisma aka Skizzz, is a member of the Canadian hip-hop collective Monolith. In addition to appearing on Monolith records, he has become a frequent producer for fellow Toronto artists including Dan-e-o and J Shiltz.
