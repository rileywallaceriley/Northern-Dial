sazon diamante

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Sazon Diamante was an East Vancouver rapper and an early Latin voice in Canadian hip-hop. A childhood friend and collaborator of Concise and the younger brother of Rascalz producer DJ Kemo, he appeared with the Rascalz and Notch on the 2002 single Crazy World before his death, leaving a lasting connection to Vancouver's hip-hop history.
