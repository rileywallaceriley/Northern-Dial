asia

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

asia is a Toronto underground hip-hop artist represented on the 1998 local compilation Invading the Mega City. Northern Dial carries the artist's track 'Dedicated' from that release. The compilation was built around Toronto-based independent rap artists and documents the city's late-1990s hip-hop scene.
