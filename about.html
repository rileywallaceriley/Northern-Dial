About Northern Dial

Canadian Music Deserves Context

Northern Dial is an independent Canadian music discovery, documentation and radio project. We use curated programming, source-backed artist information, editorial storytelling and digital discovery tools to help listeners find Canadian music beyond the handful of names that dominate mainstream recommendation systems.

Our Mandate

Discovery. Documentation. Dissemination.

Northern Dial exists to make Canadian music easier to find, understand and share, with particular attention to independent, emerging, regional and under-documented artists. Radio is the front door, but the larger project is about building useful public infrastructure around Canadian music.

Discovery

Connect listeners with Canadian artists through 24/7 programming, requests and Northern Dial Discovery.

Documentation

Build source-backed artist profiles that add biography, location, official links and context to the music in the catalogue.

Dissemination

Give Canadian music more ways to travel through radio, editorial features, searchable artist pages and future live programming.

How We Work

Human Curation, Source-Backed Profiles

Northern Dial does not treat every database credit as fact. Artist identities are reviewed against reliable sources, non-Canadian artists and non-artist credits are removed when the evidence is clear, ambiguous cases stay unresolved rather than becoming guesses and profiles are enriched with citations, official links and location information where available.

This editorial layer matters because the artist library also powers Northern Dial Discovery. The goal is not simply to collect names, but to make the catalogue trustworthy enough to support meaningful public discovery.

Current Structure

Riley-Led, Collaborator-Supported

Riley Wallace, founder and editor, leads Northern Dial's mandate, editorial standards, product direction, research and grant delivery. Northern Dial is currently an independent Riley-led project and is not presently representing itself as an incorporated nonprofit.

Project work can be supported by independent collaborators organized around three functions: development, partnerships and audience growth and design and curation. These are collaborator functions rather than named permanent staff positions and are filled as project needs and funding allow.

Open-Source Community Project

The codebase is public and the long-term vision is collaborative: more regional voices, more artist context, more ways to discover Canadian music and more opportunities for contributors to help build the platform.

Accessibility

Access Is Part of the Product

Northern Dial is working toward an experience that is usable across devices, connection speeds and abilities. Accessibility considerations include semantic page structure, keyboard-friendly controls, readable contrast, meaningful image descriptions, mobile layouts and reduced-motion support. Future audio and interview formats will prioritize captions or transcripts where applicable.
