New Singles in the Deck: 12 Tracks in Rotation Right Now

From Montréal R&B and francophone pop to Toronto indie, Afro-house and Okanagan pop, these are 12 recent additions getting spins on Northern Dial.

Northern Dial artwork

The latest Northern Dial adds stretch from artists returning after years away to musicians still building their first project. The common thread is simpler: each record earned a place in rotation, and each opens a different route into the Canadian music library.

01 Monsune · “Sure”

Toronto, Ontario · Indie pop / soul

Scott Zhang has released very little music as Monsune since the 2019 EP Tradition, but he did not disappear from record-making. His production credits since then include Drake's “Race My Mind,” while “OUTTA MY MIND” and “Mountain” kept his own small catalogue circulating well beyond its release cycle.

“Sure,” released Aug. 24, puts Zhang back at the centre of a Monsune record after several years focused largely on production. The song runs a little over four minutes and carries the layered pop and soul instincts that made Tradition stick without trying to recreate that EP.

02 ANJXLXE x Raccoon · “Rêve un peu”

Montréal, Québec · R&B / pop / rap

ANJXLXE and Montréal rapper Raccoon approach ambition from opposite sides on “Rêve un peu.” ANJXLXE frames the record around continuing to dream when comparison and outside judgment make shrinking yourself feel easier, while Raccoon's verse turns toward online hate, rage bait and the impulse to resent somebody else's progress.

Da-P produced the 2:27 track, giving the pair a light R&B and pop foundation without sanding down the rap element. Anne-Julie Lemieux and Shamyr Daléus-Louis share the writing credit, with Pharenz Abellard credited for the music.

Meet ANJXLXEMeet Raccoon

03 Colin Léo · “Mêmes habitudes”

Montréal, Québec · Alternative / francophone pop

Colin Léo built “Mêmes habitudes” around the Penrose staircase, an impossible structure that appears to keep climbing while eventually returning to its starting point. The Montréal singer-songwriter uses that loop to describe a relationship repeating the same failures while the arrangement keeps moving around it.

Producer and composer Pavel Navarro shifts the track through ambient synths, sharper hyperpop sections, distorted drums and a Wurlitzer-led finish. Félix Paul of Rau_Ze adds violin and synth arrangements, extending Colin Léo's mix of rap writing, R&B, pop and Québec alternative music.

Meet Colin LéoRequest Colin Léo

04 KARABA x KMNI · “LMHYS”

Montréal, Québec · Afro-house / club

KARABA spent years as a professional dancer before shifting her focus to DJing and production in 2016. That background still sits inside the Montréal-raised artist's Afro-house records, which are built around rhythm, physical response and the kind of transitions that make sense in a room before they make sense on paper.

“LMHYS,” her current collaboration with KMNI, keeps that focus on the dance floor. It follows an active 2026 run for KARABA that has included new singles, festival appearances and international club dates.

Meet KARABARequest KARABA

05 Mulan · “What Am I”

Montréal, Québec · R&B / pop

“What Am I” opens Angelic Crashout, the five-track debut project Montréal singer-songwriter Mulan released on Sept. 18. The EP moves from the opener into “Supermodel Boy,” “Butterfly,” the title track and “Outgrew You,” giving Mulan enough room to move between R&B and brighter pop writing without locking the project into one sound.

“Supermodel Boy” leans more melodic, “Butterfly” is built around a more immediate hook and the title track pushes into darker territory. “What Am I” works as the front door, but the rest of Angelic Crashout gives a broader picture of where Mulan is starting.

Meet MulanRequest Mulan

06 Rosabell · “Flowers”

Toronto, Ontario · Pop / R&B

Toronto-based Nigerian-Canadian singer-songwriter Rosabell centres “Flowers” on the point where patience stops being generosity. The song turns a relationship stuck in repetition into a decision to stop measuring self-worth through somebody else's attention.

The Arkeyys-produced track is the title song from Rosabell's three-song debut EP In Bloom. Its brighter, dance-leaning production gives the record movement without softening what Rosabell is actually writing about.

Meet RosabellRequest Rosabell

07 AHSIA · “SO NICE”

Vancouver, British Columbia · R&B / soul

Vancouver artist AHSIA produced “SO NICE” herself, with Sebera Arnaud Hirwa contributing acoustic guitar, engineering and additional production and Yamil Chain-Haddad on bass. The 4:31 track was released independently through AHSIA Records on Aug. 21.

That personnel fits the wider AHSIA catalogue, which moves through R&B, soul, hip-hop and jazz without treating any one of them as a permanent box. “SO NICE” gives the arrangement time to settle rather than rushing toward a hook.

Meet AHSIARequest AHSIA

08 KAYAM · “Singing In The Rain”

Calgary, Alberta · R&B / pop

Calgary singer-songwriter KAYAM released “Singing In The Rain” on Aug. 28. Qaim Khalfan is credited as lyricist and composer, with Emmanuelito Canillo also credited as composer, and the track has already moved into Canadian radio programming beyond KAYAM's own channels.

The single sits comfortably inside a catalogue that has crossed pop, R&B and Afrobeats. It is polished enough to read quickly on radio while still functioning as an introduction to an artist whose wider audience is being built in real time.

Meet KAYAMRequest KAYAM

09 Olivia Penalva · “Distance Makes a Love Story”

Vernon, British Columbia · Pop

Olivia Penalva has been releasing music since her teens, which gives “Distance Makes a Love Story” a different context from a typical emerging-pop single. The Vernon singer has more than a decade of public catalogue behind her even though she is still in her mid-20s.

Released Feb. 13, the song was written by Penalva and producer Tavish Crowe. It looks at the way distance can clean up a relationship in memory, leaving the version you miss competing with the one you actually lived.

Meet Olivia PenalvaRequest Olivia Penalva

10 AVIV · “Mind Candy”

Toronto, Ontario · Alternative pop

AVIV builds “Mind Candy” around the difference between being loved as a person and being easier to love as an idea. The Toronto artist keeps returning to the version of somebody that exists inside another person's head, where attraction can stay uncomplicated because the real person has not interrupted the fantasy yet.

The September single pairs that idea with a lighter surface than the subject suggests. Its official visualizer arrived Sept. 10, giving the song a straightforward entry point into AVIV's current run of releases.

Meet AVIVRequest AVIV

11 Ada Lea feat. MEGGO · “the evergreen, the birch”

Montréal, Québec · Indie folk / alternative

Ada Lea, the recording project of Montréal songwriter and visual artist Alexandra Levy, released “the evergreen, the birch” as part of the seven-track EP the end is a wave. Levy assembled the project from material written at different points rather than forcing the songs into one chronological period.

MEGGO adds layered harmonies to “the evergreen, the birch,” while the acoustic arrangement leaves space for Levy's free-associative writing. Levy has described the song as a watery collage and a walk through the woods, which is about as close as the track gets to explaining its own logic.

Meet Ada LeaRequest Ada Lea

12 TÖME · “MR MONEY”

Montréal-born · Afrofusion / R&B

Montréal-born Nigerian-French Canadian artist TÖME has built her catalogue around Afrofusion, with R&B, pop and hip-hop moving through it rather than sitting in separate lanes. “MR MONEY” continues that approach in a 2026 run that keeps her Canadian roots connected to a much broader musical geography.

The track is one of TÖME's current releases being pushed across her artist channels, and it sits naturally beside the cross-border collaborations and genre mixing that already define her catalogue.
