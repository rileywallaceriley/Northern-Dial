Rochelle Jordan Expands Through The Wall With Disc 2

September 11, 2026

Rochelle Jordan is pushing Through The Wall further onto the dancefloor with Disc 2, the second installment in her five-part remix series.

The Toronto-raised British-Canadian singer, songwriter and producer has handed four tracks from her acclaimed 2025 album to a stacked group of producers. Disc 2 includes Chad Hugo’s remix of “Never Enough,” Terry Hunter’s take on “Doing It Too” and two new versions of “Around” from Shanti Celeste and Bianca Oblivion. The EP arrived Sept. 10 via EMPIRE and follows Disc 1, which featured Honey Dijon, Ron Trent, KLSH and Halogenix.

“Through The Wall lives at the intersection of different sounds, so it felt natural to open that world up to artists whose perspectives I deeply respect. Chad Hugo, Terry Hunter, Bianca Oblivion and Shanti Celeste each pulled something different and beautiful from these records, expanding the language of these album tracks while still honoring its core.”

Rochelle Jordan

The larger series is set to continue with three more discs through December, turning Through The Wall into an evolving showcase for the house, R&B and electronic scenes already embedded in Jordan’s sound.

Jordan has spent more than a decade moving between R&B and club music, but this remix run makes that relationship explicit. Disc 2 feels less like an appendix to the album than a second life for it.
