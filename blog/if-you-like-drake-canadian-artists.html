If You Like Drake, Listen to These 5 Canadian Artists

Drake has spent years moving between rap, melody and late-night R&B. These five Canadian artists work in some of the same territory without sounding like substitutes.

Northern Dial artwork

This isn't a list of Drake soundalikes. Start with the part of his catalogue you return to most, then follow that thread somewhere else in Canadian music.

For sharp rap writing and melodic control

AR Paisley

Mississauga's AR Paisley can move from clipped bars into melody without making the switch feel like a feature request. His writing is direct, confident and built for replay. If you like Drake when he is rapping first but still thinking about the hook, Paisley is an easy next stop.

For the after-hours R&B side

FRVRFRIDAY

FRVRFRIDAY's records live comfortably in the space between R&B and melodic rap: restrained vocals, hazy production and songs that work better after midnight than at noon. His 2026 single “BORN WINNER,” featuring Boi-1da, keeps that mood intact while giving the drums more weight.

Explore FRVRFRIDAY's Northern Dial profile →

For introspection with more soul in the frame

TOBi

TOBi is a stronger fit for listeners who come to Drake for self-examination rather than mood alone. The Toronto-area artist is a rapper and singer with a soul vocabulary underneath the writing. His 2026 For Good Measure live sessions put the voice and the words in front, especially on “Hoodwinked.”

Explore TOBi's Northern Dial profile →

For R&B that keeps the edges loose

LOONY

LOONY comes at Toronto R&B from a different angle. Her songs leave room for awkward pauses, emotional reversals and vocal choices that feel conversational instead of immaculate. “RaGDOLL,” released in July 2026, is a good recent entry point if you like Drake's quieter R&B records but want something less polished around the edges.

Explore LOONY's Northern Dial profile →

For GTA melodic rap and the Boi-1da connection

NorthSideBenji

NorthSideBenji has one of the clearest bridges back to the Toronto rap ecosystem Drake helped make global. His delivery stays low-key even when the writing turns tense, and melody usually arrives without softening the record. “TRYNA WIN,” made with Boi-1da as part of Canada Soccer's 2026 music project, is a useful recent starting point.

For a recent visual from Benji, “Harder To Please” shows the quieter, more inward side of the same lane.
