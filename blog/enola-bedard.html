Enola Bédard Finds Her Voice in Motion on “Unavez”

September 3, 2026

Some songs feel like they are already moving before the first verse arrives. Enola Bédard’s “Unavez” has that quality: a warm, rhythmic pop track shaped by the tension between wanting to speak and waiting for the right moment.

Bédard is a Canadian artist from Quebec City who first built a global audience through dance. “Unavez,” released in 2026 through DOOGOOD, gives that movement a new form. The song’s pulse is physical, but its subject is quieter—the uncertainty that sits between attraction, vulnerability and an honest conversation.

The track moves through French, English and Spanish, reflecting Bédard’s international outlook without treating its multilingual writing as a gimmick. The languages work as part of the song’s texture, shifting the feeling without interrupting its pop shape.

That balance is central to “Unavez.” The production stays light on its feet, while the writing holds back from forcing a neat conclusion. There is anticipation here rather than melodrama: the moment when two people understand what is happening but neither has said it out loud.

Before stepping into music, Bédard became known for dance videos and built an audience of more than 16 million followers on TikTok, according to coverage from BabyStep Magazine and Bong Mines Entertainment. “Unavez” does not abandon that history. It translates it. The song treats rhythm as more than a backdrop; movement is part of how the track communicates.

Her studio work has also included collaborators Nick Monson and Chaz Mishan, according to BabyStep Magazine. That places “Unavez” within a broader pop-making process, but the song’s identity remains clear: a multilingual release built around motion, restraint and emotional uncertainty.

For Northern Dial listeners, “Unavez” is a good place to begin with Bédard’s music. It introduces an artist moving from a career built visibly through choreography into a pop catalogue where voice, rhythm and visual storytelling can occupy the same space.
