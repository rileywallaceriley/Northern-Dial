Long-Term Project in Development
Canadian Music Archive and Atlas

Artists in Place, Time and Context

Northern Dial's long-term digital roadmap is a searchable, explorable Canadian music resource connecting artists with geography, eras, scenes, releases, stories and source provenance. The current artist library is the foundation for that work, but it is not being represented as a preservation archive yet.

The Opportunity

Canadian Music Is More Than a List of Names

Streaming services are good at making music available. They are less useful at explaining how artists connect to a city, a scene, a decade, a community or one another. Northern Dial is interested in building that missing layer of public context.

The Archive and Atlas concept would extend the current catalogue from artist-by-artist documentation into a navigable cultural resource.

Potential Content Layers

From Catalogue to Cultural Map

Artists

Source-backed biographies, official links, locations, active periods, musical relationships and Northern Dial coverage.

Places and Scenes

Connections between artists and cities, provinces, local movements, venues, communities and regional histories.

Eras and Releases

Ways to explore Canadian music by decade, active years, releases and changing musical contexts.

Stories and Oral Histories

Future interviews, essays, firsthand accounts and contextual storytelling that document more than metadata alone.

Source Provenance

Clear links back to reliable sources so users can distinguish documented information from inference or unresolved research.

Discovery Paths

Interactive routes through genre, place, era and related artists that turn documentation into active public discovery.

Design Principles

Built for Public Use

01

Source-Backed

Ambiguous information stays unresolved until evidence supports it. Provenance should remain visible.

02

Accessible

Mobile usability, keyboard navigation, meaningful image descriptions, readable contrast and low-bandwidth access should be considered from the start.

03

Sustainable

The project should be maintainable over time, with structured metadata and workflows that do not depend on one-off manual pages.

Important distinction: Northern Dial currently documents and contextualizes artists. Long-term preservation, archival stewardship and oral-history work would require additional processes, partnerships and resources. This roadmap is intended to build toward that standard rather than overclaim it today.
